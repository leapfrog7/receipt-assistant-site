From email to an eOffice receipt

Email Diarisation Assistant provides a side panel for supported Government Mail and eFile deployments. Open an email, edit the receipt subject if needed, choose Email only, Email with attachments, or Attachments only, then start automatic receipt creation.

Email pages use webmail's print layout; selected PDF attachments are processed locally. Your receipt action uploads the prepared document to your configured eOffice service and generates the receipt.

Requires authorised access to compatible mail/eOffice services. A configured URL does not add support for a different application. This is independent software, not an official or endorsed Government product.
