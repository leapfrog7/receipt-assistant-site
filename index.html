From email to an eOffice receipt

Email Diarisation Assistant provides a side panel for supported Government Mail and eFile deployments. Open an email, edit the receipt subject if needed, choose email only or select PDF attachments, then start automatic receipt creation.

Preparation uses webmail?s print layout and processes PDFs locally. Your receipt action uploads the prepared document to your configured eOffice service and generates the receipt.

Requires authorised access to compatible mail/eOffice services. A configured URL does not add support for a different application. This is independent software, not an official or endorsed Government product.
