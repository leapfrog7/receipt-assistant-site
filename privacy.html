Privacy policy

Publisher: Undersigned - Effective date: 9 November 2026 - Applies to Email Diarisation Assistant, also called Receipt Assistant.

The extension handles email content on your device. If you choose to create a receipt, it uploads the prepared PDF and receipt fields to your configured eOffice service and clicks Generate automatically. It does not send your correspondence to the publisher.

Information the extension handles

  • Content of the email you open or explicitly select: subject, sender, recipients, displayed date, body and quoted or forwarded material available in the mail interface.
  • Attachment names, sizes and PDF indicators, and the contents of PDF attachments you select for a combined package. Other attachment types are not converted.
  • Configured mail/eOffice addresses and relevant open-tab URL, origin, frame, loading and page-structure information needed to find the workspace, validate the selected message and recognise session expiry.
  • Your edited receipt subject, PDF selection and workflow status. Receipt fields may contain personal or sensitive information present in the source correspondence.
  • Local interaction information needed to operate native Print, attachment Download, upload and Generate controls. Optional, user-started support diagnostics may observe new browser download events to identify matching mail-origin events.

Purpose and automatic actions

These data are used only to recognise the open email, let you choose a source message and PDF attachments, prepare the selected email and/or PDF attachments (using webmail's print layout when email pages are included), save a requested local copy, and create the receipt you request. Starting receipt creation authorises automatic eOffice navigation, field filling, PDF upload and one Generate attempt. It does not authorise Generate & Send, Generate & Copy, or forwarding a receipt. Repeating an uncertain Generate is not automatic.

Where data go

Email/PDF preparation runs locally in the browser. The extension interacts with your configured mail service using your existing signed-in browser session. On your receipt-creation action, the prepared PDF and form fields are sent over HTTPS to your configured eOffice service. Those services process and retain data under their own applicable policies. There is no publisher-operated processing server, advertising service, analytics collector or AI service in the extension.

We do not sell user data, use it for advertising, or transfer it for unrelated purposes or creditworthiness/lending decisions. Data use and transfer are limited to the extension's disclosed purpose and comply with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use restrictions.

Storage and retention

  • Workspace URL/session-check settings are stored in Chrome's local extension storage. Explicit hostname/category preferences used by diagnostics are also stored locally. The extension does not sync these settings to a publisher server.
  • Chrome session storage may retain a readiness status, check time and configured eFile origin/application path. A readiness result is accepted for at most 15 minutes; the stored record is removed or replaced on invalidation/rechecking or when the browser session ends. If you arrange the app companion beside Mail, it also retains window/tab identifiers, positions, sizes and normal/maximised state for restoring that layout. These records contain no correspondence or credentials, stay on this device, and expire or are cleared when no longer needed or the browser session ends.
  • Mail previews, source selections, diagnostics and PDF preparation data are temporary memory. A prepared receipt PDF is available for reuse for up to 15 minutes and can disappear sooner when cleared, replaced or when the extension/browser process restarts. Closing the side panel does not necessarily stop a preparation already running in the background.
  • Downloaded files remain on your device until you delete them. Receipts already uploaded or generated remain in eOffice according to that service's rules; clearing or uninstalling the extension does not remove them.

Permissions and authentication

Chrome site access is requested for the exact configured hosts. Optional Government domain families are declared to support ministry deployments; the extension's routing and automation checks accept only the configured origins. The debugger permission is used to generate a PDF from a matched webmail print-preview target, then detach; it is not used to inspect network traffic or credentials. Downloads access is optional for local saving and explicitly started download diagnostics. Storage saves preferences and the limited session/readiness and window-layout records described above; sidePanel and declarativeContent provide the workspace-specific interface; activeTab and scripting support the chosen page.

The extension does not read password-field values, extract authentication cookies, store account passwords, sign in for you or keep sessions alive. Existing browser authentication may accompany requests to your configured services. When you invoke the assistant, explicitly retry a check, or return after the readiness period expires, it may open a temporary inactive tab at your configured eFile inbox to confirm module access using the existing browser session. This can contact that eOffice service before you choose to create a receipt. Unchanged successful temporary checks are closed; sign-in/recovery pages may remain available. A portal sign-in alone does not confirm eFile access. The optional periodic status check reads only already-open configured tabs; it does not open or refresh them. There is no automatic sign-in or scheduled keep-alive request.

App window controls

In the standalone mail app, the extension reuses one companion window. Arrange beside mail is an explicit request to resize the linked workspace and assistant on the current display; Restore layout and Hide assistant use the temporary geometry record to restore only unchanged owned positions. Manual window changes are respected. Hiding keeps the current assistant document and choices; closing it can clear them.

Diagnostics and support

Copied diagnostic reports use structural counts and fixed status/failure categories. They exclude correspondence, URLs, file paths, filenames, receipt numbers, credentials and raw browser errors. Reports are not sent automatically. If you email support, the publisher receives what you voluntarily send and uses it to respond and troubleshoot. Do not send live official correspondence, credentials or sensitive attachments. Redact screenshots and use synthetic examples.

Your choices

You can edit workspace settings, turn off optional eOffice checks, choose which PDFs to include, clear preparation data, revoke site/API permissions in Chrome, or uninstall the extension. Uninstalling removes local extension settings; delete downloaded files separately. Use the relevant mail/eOffice service for its retained records and access controls.
