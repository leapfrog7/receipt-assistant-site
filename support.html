Download, installation and update instructions

Before using the extension

Use an authorised account and a compatible Government Mail/eFile deployment. Configure your workspace URLs in Settings and grant Chrome access to the exact hosts. Mail and eOffice sign-ins expire independently. After signing in to the eOffice portal, launch the eFile module, wait for its inbox and choose Check again. Optional session status is a structural snapshot; actual receipt preparation revalidates the form.

When preparation stops

  1. If Generate may already have been attempted, inspect eOffice first. Do not start another receipt merely because the side panel has not yet confirmed completion.
  2. For a signed-out session, sign in to the relevant service. A retained prepared PDF expires after at most 15 minutes or earlier if the browser/extension restarts.
  3. On a changed mail view, refresh recognition and choose the intended source message again.
  4. For a native Print failure, check Chrome popup restrictions and whether DevTools or browser policy prevents the scoped PDF operation.
  5. Use Support & diagnostics to copy the sanitised report. Include extension version, Chrome version and a short description of the stage that stopped.

Use synthetic examples. Never email passwords, authentication links, live official correspondence or sensitive attachments to support. A generated receipt is a real record in your configured service; the extension cannot undo it.
